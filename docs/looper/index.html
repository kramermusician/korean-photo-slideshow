LoopGrid Web

A video looper in the browser. Space records a camera-and-audio take, space again closes it into a loop that plays at once, in time with the rest. Up to nine loops fill the grid.

  • Works in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari on a laptop or desktop. Chrome or Edge is the smoothest.
  • Use headphones, or the speakers will feed back into the next loop.
  • Keep this tab in front while you play. A background tab stops drawing frames.
  • Nothing leaves your computer. Loops live in memory and vanish when you close the tab.

How to play

Press space to start a take, and space again to close it. The first loop sets the length; every later take snaps to a quarter, a half, or a whole number of that length, so everything stays in phase. Number keys mute tiles. Tap T a few times before the first loop to set a tempo, then M for a click and C for a one-bar count-in.

If the picture lands a little after the sound, record a clap and nudge the camera offset with [ and ] until they line up. The setting is remembered.

Settings

The frame size sets how much memory each loop takes: about 7, 15 or 28 MB per second of loop. The cap is 1.5 GB, shown in the top bar.

Audio input is captured raw: echo cancellation, noise suppression and auto gain are off, so instruments come through as they are.
